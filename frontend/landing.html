Clinical Decision Architecture

Multiclass Risk Stratification
Trained on clinical cohort data using an optimized gradient-boosted classifier (XGBoost) to stratify patients into Healthy/Normal, Insomnia, and Sleep Apnea with 93% accuracy on stratified test splits.
TreeSHAP Local Attributions
Computes exact game-theoretic Shapley values per patient. Every classification decision displays the top 3 driving biometric factors and their directional impact on risk, compliant with EU AI Act and FDA SAMD transparency criteria.
Cardiovascular Biomarker (Pulse Pressure)
Continuously computes arterial Pulse Pressure (Systolic BP - Diastolic BP). Readings exceeding 50 mmHg trigger isolated systolic hypertension and arterial stiffness clinical alerts.
Clinical NLP Symptom Screener
Rule-based natural language screener for patient sleep journals. Automatically identifies nocturnal airway disruption indicators (choking, gasping), hyperarousal, and morning cephalalgia.
Wearable & Google Fit Telemetry Ingestion
Supports standard time-series data imports from Google Health Connect, Apple Health, and multi-day CSV export streams with automatic feature extraction.
Longitudinal Patient Database
Embedded local storage recording daily vital time-series, calculated WHO BMI indexes, and rolling 7-day arterial compliance averages per registered patient ID.
